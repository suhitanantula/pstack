# Perform — Move with Intention

**Skill id:** `pstack-perform`  
**Artefact:** Live Move Brief + Execution Evidence  

## Participant question

What is the smallest intentional move that lets reality answer us?

## Purpose

Turn insight into a **bounded experiment or intervention**. Speed without unexamined automation.

## Agent helps

- Shape a small, testable move  
- Define done / useful / fail  
- Structure execution notes and evidence  
- Keep scope cuttable  

## Human must contribute

- Strategic intent  
- Trust and irreversibility boundaries  
- Permission and ethics  
- What “good enough” means  

## Protocol

1. **Name the move** in one sentence.  
2. **Bound it:** time, people, cost, reversibility.  
3. **Success signal** and **kill / stop signal**.  
4. **Do or simulate** within agreed boundaries.  
5. **Capture evidence** (what happened, not what we hoped).  

## Output contract

Copy `artifacts/live-move.md`:

- Move statement  
- Boundaries  
- What we did  
- What reality said  
- Implications for Portfolio / Pause  

## Completion signal

Something real was tried (or rigorously simulated) and evidence is written down.

## Loop back when

Evidence invalidates framing → Perceive. Evidence opens new paths → Portfolio. Need a call now → Pause.

## Learning evidence

Participant can move without turning speed into unexamined automation.
