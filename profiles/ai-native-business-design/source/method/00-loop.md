# The PStack Loop

## Core stance

The 5Ps are a **recursive co-intelligence loop**, not a stage-gate ladder.

Looping is normal. Returning to an earlier P is how the system gets smarter — not failure.

## The five steps

| P | Tagline | Question |
|---|---------|----------|
| **Perceive** | Never Forget the People | What is actually happening, and what are we not seeing? |
| **Perform** | Move with Intention | What is the smallest intentional move that lets reality answer? |
| **Portfolio** | Designing for Anti-Fragility | What options are alive now that we have learned? |
| **Pause/Promote** | Choose What Matters | What do we promote, preserve, or stop — and why? |
| **Progress** | Build Learning Systems | What do we know now that should change the next loop? |

**Observer** sits above the loop: personal practice development and (with consent) course-level pattern learning.

## Common return paths

- Pause/Promote → Portfolio — recommendation weak; need better options  
- Portfolio → Perceive — options reveal wrong framing  
- Perform → Pause/Promote — reality changed the bet  
- Perform → Perceive — experiment showed the problem was wrong  
- Progress → Perceive — outcomes rewrote the map  

## Co-intelligence

Each P is a zone of collaboration:

| | Humans | AI |
|--|--------|-----|
| Bring | Context, values, taste, politics, accountability | Pattern scan, rapid variation, synthesis, structure |
| Risk | Over-owning without evidence | Fluency without judgement |

Shared question every turn: **Whose judgement improves this P right now?**

## Portfolio is always active

Portfolio is a **lens**, not a single gate:

- In Perceive, frames are forming  
- In Perform, you test one option  
- In Pause, you draw from the field  
- After Progress, you update the field  

Never collapse to one option before Pause has done its work — unless the move is deliberately exploratory and reversible.

## Prototype before you present

If you cannot make a rough working prototype (sketch, scenario, mock, experiment design), the idea is not ready for a big decision.

## Strategic rhythm, not AI strategy

You do not need a separate “AI strategy document.” You need a **rhythm** for sensing, moving, holding options, deciding, and learning — with AI inside every step.
