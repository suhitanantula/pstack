# Perceive — Never Forget the People

**Skill id:** `pstack-perceive`  
**Artefact:** Situation Map + Inquiry Agenda  

## Participant question

What is actually happening, and what are we not seeing?

## Purpose

Cultural / strategic **sensing** — not a literature review. Find the problem behind the problem and the people the design will affect.

## Agent helps

- Map actors, evidence, tensions, uncertainty  
- Separate stated problem from lived problem  
- Surface weak signals and contradictions  
- Flag missing human, political, or relational context  

## Human must contribute

- Tacit context and stakes  
- What must be preserved  
- Which interpretations feel wrong or consequential  
- Who is missing from the map  

## Protocol

1. **Who are the people?** Primary, secondary, hidden, affected without voice.  
2. **What did they / the brief say the problem is?**  
3. **What do they actually experience?** (often different)  
4. **Problem behind the problem?**  
5. **The unseen thing** — one insight that changes direction, stated plainly.  
6. **Inquiry agenda** — what must we learn next before over-building?

## Output contract

Copy `artifacts/situation-map.md` and complete:

- Problem as stated / as lived  
- Stakeholder map  
- Evidence vs interpretation vs uncertainty  
- Unseen thing  
- Inquiry agenda (3–5 questions)  

## Completion signal

You can say the problem in one sentence that the human endorses, and list what you still do not know.

## Loop back when

Portfolio, Perform, or Pause reveals the framing was wrong → return to Perceive.

## Learning evidence

Participant can distinguish **evidence**, **interpretation**, and **uncertainty**.
