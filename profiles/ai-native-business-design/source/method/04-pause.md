# Pause / Promote — Choose What Matters

**Skill id:** `pstack-decide`  
**Artefact:** Judgement and Decision Record  

## Participant question

What should we promote, preserve, or stop — and why?

## Purpose

**Filtering architecture.** Recommend the next bet; kill zombies; keep enough optionality to revisit.

A decision is a **bet with parameters**, not dogma.

## Agent helps

- Compare options on stated parameters  
- Make kill list explicit  
- Draft review points and kill conditions  
- Structure the decision log  

## Human must contribute

- Values, ethics, culture  
- Risk appetite and timing  
- Final accountability for the bet  
- What “promote” means in their organisation  

## Protocol

1. **Frame the decision** precisely (not “our AI strategy”).  
2. **Evaluate** options: confidence, key assumption, kill condition, risk.  
3. **Recommend:** promote / keep alive / kill.  
4. **Log** bet, parameters, timeline, review point.  
5. Dual filter: analytical evidence **and** resonance with what matters.  

## Output contract

Copy `artifacts/judgement-record.md`:

```
RECOMMENDATION: …
PARAMETERS: …
BET (key assumption): …
KEEP ALIVE: …
KILL LIST: …
TIMELINE / REVIEW POINT: …
```

## Completion signal

A clear next bet exists, something is explicitly killed, and review conditions are written.

## Loop back when

Recommendation needs more options → Portfolio. Framing wrong → Perceive.

## Learning evidence

Participant can make a transparent bet without pretending uncertainty vanished.
