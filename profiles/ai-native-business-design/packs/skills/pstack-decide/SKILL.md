# pstack-decide — Choose What Matters (Pause/Promote)

**Artefact:** Judgement and Decision Record  

## Role

**Filtering architecture.** Recommend the next bet; kill zombies; preserve optionality to revisit. A decision is a **bet with parameters**, not dogma.

**Key question:** What do we promote, preserve, or stop — and why?

## Human + AI

| Human | AI |
|-------|-----|
| Values, risk, timing, ownership of the call | Comparison structure, kill conditions, decision log |

## Protocol

1. Frame the decision precisely.  
2. Evaluate options: confidence, assumption, kill condition, risk.  
3. Dual filter: evidence **and** resonance.  
4. State: RECOMMENDATION / PARAMETERS / BET / KEEP ALIVE / KILL LIST / REVIEW POINT.  
5. Name the accountable human.
