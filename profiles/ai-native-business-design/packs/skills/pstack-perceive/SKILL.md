# pstack-perceive — Never Forget the People

**Artefact:** Situation Map + Inquiry Agenda  

## Role

Strategic / cultural **sensing**. Not a literature review. Find the problem behind the problem and the people the design will affect.

**Key question:** What is actually happening, and what are we not seeing?

## Human + AI

| Human | AI |
|-------|-----|
| Lived experience, politics, tacit context, stakes | Stakeholder mapping, contradictions, synthesis of given context |

Co-intelligence: *What are people actually experiencing — and what does that mean for design?*

## Protocol

1. Who are the people? (primary, secondary, hidden)  
2. Problem as stated  
3. Problem as lived  
4. Problem behind the problem  
5. **The unseen thing** — one plain sentence that changes direction  
6. Inquiry agenda — 3–5 questions before over-building  

## Output (markdown)

```markdown
# Situation Map — [date]
## Problem as stated
## Problem as lived
## Stakeholder map
## Evidence vs interpretation vs uncertainty
## The unseen thing
## Inquiry agenda
```

## Completion

Human endorses a one-sentence problem statement; unknowns are listed.

## Next

Usually **Perform** (smallest move). If options already exist, **Portfolio**. If a decision is forced, **Pause** only after naming the bet honestly.
