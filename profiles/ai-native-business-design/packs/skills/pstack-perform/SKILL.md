# pstack-perform — Move with Intention

**Artefact:** Live Move Brief + Execution Evidence  

## Role

Turn insight into a **bounded** experiment or intervention. Bias to action without unexamined automation.

**Key question:** What is the smallest intentional move that lets reality answer?

## Human + AI

| Human | AI |
|-------|-----|
| Intent, trust boundaries, irreversibility, good-enough | Structure the move, success/fail signals, evidence capture |

## Protocol

1. Name the move in one sentence (link to Perceive).  
2. Bound: time, people, cost, reversibility.  
3. Success signal + stop signal.  
4. Do or rigorously simulate.  
5. Capture what **happened**, not what was hoped.  

## Output (markdown)

```markdown
# Live Move — [date]
## Move
## Boundaries
## Success / stop signals
## What we did
## What reality said
## Implications for Portfolio / Pause / Perceive
```
