# pstack-portfolio — Designing for Anti-Fragility

**Artefact:** Live Portfolio of Plays  

## Role

Hold a **live field of options** as working plays. Prototype before you present. Portfolio is a lens across the whole loop, not a single gate.

**Key question:** What options are alive now that we have learned?

## Human + AI

| Human | AI |
|-------|-----|
| Taste, risk appetite, fit | Contrasting options, assumptions, failure modes |

## Protocol

1. At least **3** distinct directions (or 2 with explicit constraint).  
2. Per option: assumption, evidence, failure mode, reversibility, status.  
3. Mark active test vs keep-alive vs parked.  
4. Light prune: what you are **not** expanding into.  
5. Contrast check: not three renames of one idea.  

## Output (markdown)

```markdown
# Portfolio of Plays — [date]
## Play A / B / C (assumption, evidence, status)
## Contrast check
## Not expanding into
```

## Next

**Pause** to promote/kill. If all options share one untested assumption → **Perform**.
