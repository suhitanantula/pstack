# Privacy and consent defaults

## Defaults

- Participant challenge material is **private by default**.  
- Instructor access is **explicit and visible** (only what they submit).  
- Cohort pattern learning is **opt-in** and de-identified.  
- Raw organisational documents are **not** used for product training by default.  
- Consequential decisions remain **attributable to the participant**.  
- Retention/deletion: state course end date; participants keep their own files.

## What the course may collect

- Redacted artefacts for assessment  
- Practice pulse (tool, Ps used/skipped, stuck moment)  
- Optional 30-day continuity answer  

## What the course must not do

- Require full chat export  
- Scrape private Claude/ChatGPT history  
- Present exploratory pack as Rotman-endorsed without agreement
