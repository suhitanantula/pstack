# Facilitator guide — single loop pilot

## Your job

You are not a better chatbot. You:

1. Help participants recognise **which P** they are in.  
2. Challenge weak evidence and convenient framing.  
3. Protect **human ownership** of the bet.  
4. Notice fluency without learning.  
5. Keep challenge material private unless the participant chooses to share redacted artefacts.

## Session flow (pilot)

1. **Install** (15 min) — Claude Desktop skills or ChatGPT path; verify with Perceive prompt.  
2. **Intake** (15 min) — Strategic Learning Brief.  
3. **Perceive** (30–45 min) — demand “unseen thing” in plain language.  
4. **Perform** (30–45 min) — smallest real move or rigorous simulation.  
5. **Portfolio** (20–30 min) — force contrast.  
6. **Pause** (20–30 min) — kill list required.  
7. **Progress** (15–20 min) — next Perceive seeds.  
8. **Pulse** (5 min) — practice pulse form.

## Red flags

- Beautiful prose, no stakeholders  
- Three “options” that are the same idea  
- Decision without kill list  
- AI recommendation accepted without human parameter  
- Skipping Progress (“we’ll remember”)

## Success

Participant can restart a second loop with the pack alone.
